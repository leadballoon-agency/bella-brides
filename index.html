Frequently Asked Questions

Why do you charge for consultations?

Our £95 consultation includes a comprehensive 60-minute evaluation with written recommendations and priority booking - far more detailed than typical free assessments. This investment is fully credited toward your service, making it risk-free for serious clients while maintaining our exclusive service standards.

How far in advance should I book for wedding alterations?

We recommend 8-12 weeks before your wedding for optimal scheduling. However, we offer rush services for tighter timelines and same-day emergency service for White Glove Experience clients.

Do you work with all designer brands?

Yes, we have extensive experience with all luxury designers including Chanel, Hermès, Valentino, Vera Wang, and more. We understand brand-specific construction techniques and can maintain warranties where possible.

What makes your alterations different from others?

We apply the same bespoke techniques used in creating Savile Row suits - hand-finished seams, pattern matching, structural integrity, and up to 28 measurements for perfect fit (most alterations use only 5-7 measurements).

Can you restore vintage or antique garments?

Absolutely. Our Heritage Restoration Package uses museum-quality techniques for precious heirlooms, including rare fabric sourcing from our library of 7,000+ fabrics and archival documentation.

Do you offer men's alterations too?

Yes! Grant & O'Sullivan is primarily a men's bespoke tailor with Savile Row expertise. We offer complete alteration services for wedding parties - from his suits to her gown.
