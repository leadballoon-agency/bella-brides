Frequently Asked Questions

Is the consultation really free?

Yes, absolutely! Your initial consultation is completely free with no obligation. We'll examine your dress, discuss what you'd like to achieve, and provide a detailed quote for the work needed. This typically takes 30-45 minutes.

How far in advance should I book for wedding dress alterations?

We recommend booking 8-12 weeks before your wedding for the best experience. This allows time for multiple fittings and any adjustments needed. However, we can accommodate rush requests depending on our schedule - just let us know your timeline.

Do you work with all wedding dress designers?

Yes! We have decades of experience working with all major bridal designers including Vera Wang, Pronovias, Maggie Sottero, and boutique designers. We also specialize in vintage and heirloom wedding gowns that require extra care.

What makes your wedding dress alterations special?

We specialize exclusively in bridal wear, so we understand the unique construction of wedding dresses. Every alteration is hand-finished with meticulous attention to detail, ensuring your dress fits perfectly and you feel confident on your special day.

Can you restore my mother's or grandmother's wedding dress?

Absolutely! Vintage and heirloom wedding dress restoration is one of our specialties. We treat these precious garments with the utmost care, preserving original details while ensuring a perfect modern fit. We can also carefully clean and restore delicate vintage fabrics and lace.

How much will my alterations cost?

Every wedding dress is unique, so we provide personalized quotes after examining your dress at the free consultation. Costs depend on the complexity of alterations needed, fabric type, and embellishments. We'll give you a clear, detailed quote with no hidden fees before any work begins.
