## 🔗 Landing Page vs Lead Form

### Option A: Landing Page (Recommended)
**Pros:**
- Full control over experience
- 4-step form builds investment
- Better quality leads (more committed)
- GHL calendar integration
- Can retarget with pixel

**Cons:**
- Slightly lower conversion rate
- Requires click to website

**Setup:**
- Destination: https://bridalalterations.london#booking
- Scrolls directly to booking form
- Pixel tracking at each step

### Option B: Meta Lead Form
**Pros:**
- Higher conversion rate (easier to complete)
- Auto-fill phone/email
- No website visit required
- Mobile-optimized

**Cons:**
- Less qualified leads potentially
- No GHL calendar (manual follow-up)
- Less data for optimization

**Recommendation:** Start with Landing Page (Option A) for better quality, test Lead Form after 2 weeks if CPA is too high.

## 🚫 Meta Ads Policy Compliance

### Important Restrictions
✅ **Allowed:**
- "Free consultation"
- "Expert alterations"
- Specific services listed
- Location-based targeting
- Testimonials (with consent)

❌ **Not Allowed:**
- Body-shaming language
- "Before/after" medical claims
- Exaggerated urgency ("Last chance!")
- "Perfect" without context
- Weight loss implications
- Discrimination of any kind

### Best Practices
- Use inclusive language
- Focus on dress fit, not body shape
- Avoid "lose weight" or sizing down language
- Don't promise specific outcomes
- Include privacy policy link
- Honor "Not endorsed by Facebook" disclaimer
